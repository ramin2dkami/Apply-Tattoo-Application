# Demo script — 90 seconds

Do this on a phone. Handing them your phone is the demo; showing a laptop screen is a
presentation, and presentations don't get feedback.

## Before you start

Have one good example link already made — a real tattoo design on a forearm, sized
properly. You'll use it as the punchline, not the opener.

## The script

**Open with their problem, not our product** (15s)

> "How many messages does it usually take before you can quote someone?"

Then be quiet. Whatever they say next is worth more than the rest of the demo. Write
it down afterwards in `customers/`.

**Show the loop** (45s)

Hand them the phone with the app open, and say as little as possible:

> "Pick where you'd want it. Drop in any image. Make it the size you want."

Let them fumble. Every place they hesitate is a bug in the product, not in them. Do
not rescue them — note it.

**Land the point** (20s)

Point at the size readout:

> "That's four and a half inches. Would that have taken you a few messages to get to?"

Then show the share page:

> "That's what lands in your DMs."

**Ask the only question that matters** (10s)

> "What would have to be true for you to send this to someone tomorrow?"

## Afterwards

Write it up in `customers/[their-name].md` within the hour, in their words. Anything
that contradicts the roadmap goes in `REVIEW.md` too.

## What not to do

- Don't explain the contour warp. If they notice it, great; if they don't, it wasn't
  worth explaining.
- Don't pitch the roadmap. They'll ask for features that are already on it and you'll
  learn which ones they care about — that's the signal, and pre-empting it destroys it.
- Don't defend the illustrations when they say "that doesn't look like my client's
  arm." Ask what would be close enough. That answer decides whether photo mode moves
  up the roadmap.
