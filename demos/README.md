# demos/

Material for showing the product to a tattoo artist. Keep it embarrassingly simple:
the demo is a phone, a link, and ninety seconds.

- `demo-script.md` — what to say and in what order
- `assets/` — sample tattoo images and finished example links to demo with

The demo's job is not to impress. It's to get the artist talking about their own
inquiry pile. If you finish the demo and they haven't described their own workflow,
the demo went badly regardless of how it looked.
