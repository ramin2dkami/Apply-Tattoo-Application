# Routine — Weekly review

Once a week, or whenever the roadmap stops feeling true. Half an hour.

1. **What shipped?** Read this week's `REVIEW.md` entries. If nothing shipped, say why
   plainly — blocked, scope was wrong, or the work went somewhere else.
2. **What did artists say?** Read any new files in `customers/`. Quotes, not summaries.
3. **What did we get wrong?** Find one thing the week disproved. There's always one.
   If you can't find it, you weren't talking to enough people.
4. **Re-order the roadmap.** Move things between Now / Next / Later based on 2 and 3,
   not on what's most interesting to build. Keep **Now** small enough to finish.
5. **Check the non-goals.** Has anything crept in? Cut it or move it to Later
   deliberately, with a note.
6. **Update `context/`** if the business understanding actually changed. Add a decision
   to `context/decisions.md` if a real fork was taken — including the reasoning and
   what would reverse it.
7. **Write one `REVIEW.md` entry** covering the week.

The test of this routine: someone reading `REVIEW.md` in three months should be able
to reconstruct why the product is shaped the way it is.
