# Glossary

Words we use precisely, so specs stay unambiguous.

- **Placement** — where on the body the tattoo goes, plus its size, rotation, and
  orientation. The complete answer to "where and how big".
- **Figure** — the single neutral outline drawing of a body. There is only one.
- **Region** — a named, selectable area of the figure (forearm, calf, chest). Defined
  as a viewBox window plus a sampled silhouette, not as a separate file.
- **Chain** — a run of regions sharing one continuous surface, e.g. upper arm +
  forearm. A tattoo may span the regions within a chain but not across chains.
- **Span** — a placement covering more than one region of a chain.
- **Reference dimension** — a known real-world measurement declared on a body part
  asset (e.g. wrist-to-elbow = 26 cm). Everything scale-related derives from this.
- **Contour** — warping the tattoo image to the body part's surface so it follows the
  curve of the limb instead of sitting flat like a sticker.
- **Brief** — what the artist receives: composite image, body part, placement, and
  real-world size. The thing they quote from.
- **Share page** — the public, read-only page at the shareable link.
- **Customer** — the person getting tattooed; uses the app.
- **Artist** — the person doing the tattooing; receives the brief. The buyer.
