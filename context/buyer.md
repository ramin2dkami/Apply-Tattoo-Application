# Who we're building for

There are two people here and they are not the same person. Getting this backwards
is the easiest way to build the wrong thing.

## The buyer — the tattoo artist

- Independent artist or small studio. Books through Instagram DMs, sometimes email.
- Time-poor in a specific way: the admin around tattooing is unpaid, and inquiry
  triage is the worst of it.
- Deeply protective of their craft. Anything that looks like it's automating *taste*
  or generating art will be rejected on sight.
- Will not install software, will not create an account to try something, and will
  not ask their customers to either.
- What they want from us: fewer messages before a quote. That's it.

## The user — the customer getting tattooed

- Often nervous, often a first-timer, frequently doesn't know the vocabulary.
- Has a reference image saved on their phone already.
- Bad at estimating size in inches. Good at recognizing "that looks right."
- Will abandon anything that asks them to sign up before it shows them value.

## What this means for the app

- Zero friction on the customer side is a hard requirement, not a preference.
- The artist's first contact with the product is a link a customer sent them. It has
  to be legible in ten seconds with no onboarding.
- We make placement and sizing easy. We never touch the design itself.
